EMBER DUEL

Four elements. One table. No mercy.

Goal: reduce Ashwarden's life from 30 to 0 before yours falls.

Each turn you gain embers (mana) and draw a card. Play creatures onto your side of the table, then attack with them.

Combat: a creature deals its attack to its target; a creature target hits back. A creature cannot attack the turn it is played, unless it is Swift.

Elements: ▲ Ember beats ◆ Grove beats ■ Stone beats ● Tide beats Ember. The stronger element deals +2 attack.

Guard creatures must be attacked first. Drain heals your hero for the damage it deals. Spells strike, mend or draw.

Keys: 1–7 play a card from your hand · Space ends your turn · Esc cancels a selection.

An original game — Ember Duel. No network, no account, just the table.
